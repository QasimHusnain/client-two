# Email Studio: Rise & Crumb

This studio plans and writes email campaigns for Rise & Crumb, a small neighbourhood bakery. Brand voice lives in `brand/`, the practice lead lists in `leads/`, each campaign's plan and emails in `campaigns/`, and the layout every email must follow in `.claude/rules/emails.md`. Write in plain English, never send email to real people (only to @resend.dev test addresses or my own address), and ask me before deleting anything.
