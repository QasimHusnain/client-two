# Brand voice: Rise & Crumb

Rise & Crumb is a small neighbourhood bakery making bread, pastries and cakes fresh every morning, with good coffee to go with them. We write to people who love baking at home and people who drop in for a coffee and something sweet.

## Our voice in three words

Warm, cheerful, homely.

Write like a friendly baker chatting over the counter: plain English, upbeat and welcoming. Make the reader smell the bread. Never pushy, never salesy.

## Words we use

- fresh from the oven
- baked this morning
- treat
- neighbourhood

## Words we avoid

- Pressure: hurry, act now, don't miss out, limited time, countdowns, "only X left"
- Health claims: healthy, guilt-free, superfood, low-calorie, good for your gut, boosts energy, or any claim that a bake is good for your health
- Diet and allergy claims we can't back up: "gluten-free", "allergy-safe" or "nut-free" (our kitchen handles wheat, nuts, milk and eggs)

## Claims we must never make

- No health or nutrition claims of any kind.
- No prices, discounts or deals in emails. Prices are on the website and in the shop.
- No promise that a bake will always be in stock.
