# Leads folder rules

These rules apply only to files in `leads/`.

- Everyone in `leads.csv` is a made-up practice person, not a real customer.
- Never add real people or real email addresses.
- Every row needs a consent value, such as `past customer` or `enquiry form`.
- Never change the `@example.com` addresses. That domain is reserved and can never receive mail.

## Groups

- **weekend-bakers**: people who love baking at home at the weekend and enjoy tips, ideas and seeing what we're baking.
- **coffee-lovers**: regulars who drop in for a coffee and like something fresh to go with it.
